# 📊 Sales Analysis – Amazon Products (Power BI Project)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.
The objective of this report is to analyze Amazon product sales performance across:

* Products
* Regions
* Industries
* Customers
* Order Dates

The dashboard provides key insights into revenue, profit, quantity sold, and customer distribution to support data-driven business decisions.

# 🔎 Insights from Dashboard

## 📦 1. Product Performance Insights

* **Support** product generates the highest total profit.
* **ContactMatcher** contributes the highest quantity sold (~30% share).
* Top 4 products contribute the majority of overall quantity.
* Some products like **Storage, Big Ol Database, Marketing tools** show comparatively low profit contribution.

📌 *Business Insight:*
Focus on promoting high-margin products like Support while optimizing pricing or marketing strategies for low-performing products.

---

## 🌍 2. Regional Profit Analysis

* **AMER region** contributes the highest profit (~46%+).
* **EMEA region** follows closely (~46%).
* **APJ region** contributes significantly less (~6%).

📌 *Business Insight:*
APJ region has growth potential. Strategic expansion and targeted marketing campaigns could improve profitability in APJ.

---

## 📅 3. Sales Trend by Order Date

* Highest sales recorded on **3/18/2020 (28.1K)**.
* Sales show fluctuations across different dates.
* Recent periods show moderate but stable performance.

📌 *Business Insight:*
Sales spikes may be linked to promotions, seasonality, or campaigns. Identifying these events can help replicate success.

---

## 🏭 4. Profit by Industry

* **Finance industry** generates the highest profit (138K).
* **Energy sector** also performs strongly (95K).
* **Manufacturing** contributes moderate profit (42K).
* **Transportation & Tech** generate lower profits.

📌 *Business Insight:*
Focus more on Finance and Energy clients for premium offerings and upselling opportunities.

---

## 🗺️ 5. Global Sales Distribution

* Sales are distributed across North America, Europe, and Asia.
* Strong presence in **USA, Canada, UK, Germany, and Australia**.
* Emerging presence in South America and Africa.

📌 *Business Insight:*
North America and Europe are primary revenue drivers. Emerging markets offer future expansion opportunities.

---

## 👥 6. Customer Product Distribution

* Customers like **Allianz, Tyson Foods, Ford Motors, Siemens, Comcast** show high product purchase counts.
* Customer distribution appears fairly balanced across major clients.

📌 *Business Insight:*
Top enterprise customers contribute significantly. Strengthening B2B relationships can increase long-term revenue.
